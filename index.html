BEHIND THE LENS.

I'm Thimira Theekshana, a filmmaker, cinematographer and photographer with experience shooting across Sri Lanka and the UAE. Since 2019, I've worked on visual stories spanning events, brands, products, interiors and commercial content.

For me, every frame is an opportunity to make someone feel something. My approach brings together composition, light, movement and the details that make a story memorable.
